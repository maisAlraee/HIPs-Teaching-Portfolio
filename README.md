# High-Impact Practices Teaching Portfolio

Montgomery College | Data Science

## 1. About Me

I am a Mathematics and Data Science instructor and program advisor at Montgomery College, where I teach courses in data science, statistics, and machine learning.

My teaching philosophy focuses on active learning and connecting classroom concepts to real-world applications. I believe students learn best when they have opportunities to explore datasets, write code, solve problems, and collaborate with their peers.

I use open-source tools such as Python, R, Jupyter Notebooks and Google Colab to make learning accessible and provide students with practical skills they can apply beyond the classroom. I am also involved in curriculum development, academic advising, and student engagement initiatives. My goal is to create an inclusive learning environment where students from different backgrounds feel confident exploring Data Science and developing their technical and critical-thinking skills.

## 2. My Teaching Goals

- Help students become confident using data to answer meaningful questions.
- Create hands-on activities that connect course concepts to real situations.
- Encourage students to collaborate and explain the reasoning behind their analyses.
- Help students recognize the limits and ethical implications of data-based conclusions.


This portfolio showcases two proposed activities from the High-Impact Practices course. The diagrams are simple illustrations created for this portfolio, not visualizations of research data. The activity plans can be revised before implementation.
